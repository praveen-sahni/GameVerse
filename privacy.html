Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. GameVerse is a small hobby project — here's exactly what we store.

What we collect

  • Profile you enter: player name, avatar, favorite genre.
  • Security PIN: stored only as a one-way scrypt hash — we cannot read your PIN.
  • Gameplay: scores, best scores, XP, coins, quest completions, login times.
  • Technical: IP address and browser user-agent recorded with login sessions for abuse prevention.

What lives in your browser

Your profile copy, settings (theme, sound), streak, and quest history are kept in localStorage on your own device. Clearing site data removes them.

What we don't do

No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sales. Leaderboards show only your player name, avatar, and scores.

Deletion

Use Reset Progress in the app to wipe local data. To delete your server profile, contact the site operator — server data lives in a single SQLite file and backups rotate every 14 days.
