How to Play & Pro Tips

🐍 Snake: WASD/Arrows or swipe, P pause, eat for level up — speed ramps. Don't hit walls.
🧠 Memory: Find 8 pairs. Watch moves + stars — 3★ under 20 moves & 45s. Hint power-up available.
⭕ TicTacToe: Hard = unbeatable minimax. Win line glows. W/D/L tracked.
🎯 Blaster: Gold ★ = +22, Bomb 💣 = -12. Combo builds score. Use Slow-Mo power-up.
🏃 Runner: Space/↑ double-jump. Dust on jump. Speed ramps. R to restart.
✨ Simon: Strict resets on miss. Speed ramps every 4 levels. Repeat button available.
🧱 Breakout: Mouse, touch or ← → to move. Edge hits angle the ball. Gold bricks +30, 3 lives.
🔢 2048: Arrows, WASD, swipe or buttons. Merge matching tiles to reach 2048. Undo available.
🔥 Pro: Daily streak +20 XP, power-ups 1 per game, sound toggle top-right, theme dots, share score, per-game mini-leaderboard in modal.
